# UKO_Dashboard_KPIs

<!-- page 1 -->
This dashboard reporting framework is engineered for the UKO app , designed to serve a multinational COMESA organization. As a SaaS strategist and CEO, I have maximized the "real estate" of each of the 6 dashboard cards per module by breaking down every Major KPI into three actionable Mini KPIs (Sub-points). This structure enables an executive to see the "Health" of the business at a glance while providing the granular data needed for immediate operational intervention.
