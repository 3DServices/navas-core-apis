### A01 INSPECTA

As a driver, I want asset-type-specific pre-trip checklists, so as to inspect the right components for the right vehicle or machine. Compliance B2B/B2G

### A01 INSPECTA

As a workshop mechanic, I want failed inspection items to create repair candidates automatically, so as to shorten the time from defect discovery to workshop action. Workflow B2B

### A01 INSPECTA

As a CEO, I want inspection compliance dashboards by branch, department and asset class, so as to see where discipline is weak at scale. Executive B2B/B2G

### A01 INSPECTA

As a CEO, I want geotagged inspections to prove the driver was physically standing near the vehicle during the check, so as to prevent pencil-whipping. Governance B2B/B2G

### A02 Driver Score Card (DSC)

### A02 Driver Score Card (DSC)

As a fleet owner, I want scorecards normalized by vehicle type and duty profile, so as to compare like with like. Fairness B2B/B2G

### A02 Driver Score Card (DSC)

As a driver, I want to challenge a score event with notes or evidence, so as to explain unusual context and keep scoring credible. HIC B2B

### A02 Driver Score Card (DSC)

### A03 ECO

As a fleet manager, I want real-time harsh-driving detection for acceleration, braking, cornering, overspeeding and excessive idling, so as to intervene while risk is happening. Safety B2B/B2G

### A03 ECO

As a fuel auditor, I want ECO to quantify fuel wasted by idling, speeding, harsh driving and route inefficiency, so as to separate behavioral waste from mechanical causes. Fuel B2B

### A03 ECO

As a safety officer, I want fatigue analysis based on continuous driving time, daily rest, night driving and DMS indicators, so as to identify tired drivers early. Safety B2B/B2G

### A03 ECO

As a journey planner, I want ECO to show which routes systematically generate fatigue, harsh events and wasted fuel, so as to redesign journeys. Optimization B2B/B2G

### A04 Mafuta Card & Fuel Ops

### A04 Mafuta Card & Fuel Ops

### A04 Mafuta Card & Fuel Ops

### A05 WORKSHOP & GARAGE

### A05 WORKSHOP & GARAGE

### A05 WORKSHOP & GARAGE

### A05 WORKSHOP & GARAGE
