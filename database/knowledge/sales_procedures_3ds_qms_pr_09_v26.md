# 33.1 Glossary of Terms (Alphabetical Order)

### A
Account Manager (AM)
 The primary owner of a customer relationship in Odoo CRM; responsible for follow-up, negotiation, onboarding coordination, renewal management, churn prevention, and overall customer satisfaction.
Adoption Score
 A measure of how actively a customer is using the system — login frequency, report viewing, alerts, tickets, and feature engagement.
AI Agent
 An intelligent software agent automating parts of the sales lifecycle (lead assignment, PFI checking, renewal reminders, churn prediction, documentation audits, etc.).
Allowance
 Monthly payments for work-from-home, airtime, data, travel, meals, PPE, or accommodation.

### B
B2B / B2G / B2C
 Sales channels:
 - B2B – Business to Business
 - B2G – Business to Government
 - B2C – Business to Individual Customers
BANT
 Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline — method used to qualify leads.
Billing Plan (Wialon/Navas)
 Defines subscription pricing, features, SIM bundles, and billing cycles.

### C
CAC (Cost of Acquiring Customers)
 The total cost of acquiring one new customer, inclusive of sales, marketing, travel, and operational expenses.
Calibration (Fuel Sensors)
 Process of measuring and programming accurate fuel levels into the system using tank-specific calibration tables.
Churn
 When customers terminate or stop using the service.
Commissioning Certificate
 Final document confirming successful installation, configuration, training, and system activation.
CSO (Customer Service Officer)
 Technician responsible for installation, repairs, calibrations, and site visits.

### D
Digital-First
 Sales framework requiring initial customer engagement through digital channels (WhatsApp, email, calls, online demos) before physical meetings.
Documentation Officer
 Persona responsible for ensuring project documentation is complete and upload-ready.
DRC
 Democratic Republic of the Congo — part of regional expansion strategy.

### E
E2E (End-to-End)
 Full process cycle from lead spotting through installation and renewal.
Enterprise Solutions / ISS
 Industry-specific system bundles designed for sectors like FMCG, O&G, Construction, Mining, Transport, NGOs, etc.

### F
Fleetrun
 A Wialon module for vehicle maintenance management.
Fuel Monitoring System (FMS)
 Telematics solution for tracking and controlling vehicle fuel consumption.

### G
Geofencing
 Map areas that trigger alerts when vehicles enter or exit predefined zones.

### H
Hardware Options Matrix
 Table defining which devices (e.g., FMB920, FMC650, FMB125) match which telematics services.
Help Desk
 Customer support center handling inquiries and basic troubleshooting.

### I
IMEI
 Unique serial number for hardware devices.
Installation Schedule
 Planned time and route for installing hardware, managed by the Installation Scheduler AI Agent.
ISS (Industry Specific Solutions)
 Custom bundles of features, dashboards, sensors, and workflows tailored to each industry.

### J
Job Card
 Official form completed by CSOs during installation, including photos and signatures.

### K
KCM (Kenya Country Manager)
 Lead sales and operations manager responsible for the Kenya office.
KPI (Key Performance Indicator)
 Quantifiable measure of performance (e.g., demos, PFIs, installations, renewals, ISS add-ons).

### L
Lean Management
 Operational philosophy focused on eliminating waste, reducing unnecessary travel, increasing efficiency, and improving process flow.
Lead Spotter
 Individual who identifies a potential customer; earns commission when deal closes.
Local Purchase Order (LPO)
 Official approval by customer to purchase a product or service.

### M
Maker–Checker
 Dual-approval workflow for PFIs to ensure pricing accuracy and governance.
MTTR (Mean Time to Resolution)
 Average time taken to resolve customer service tickets and technical issues.

### N
Navas
 3D's proprietary telematics platform offering low-cost, mass-market, token-based tracking (e.g., Piki, Oliwa, Patrol, UKO, VEBA).
NPS (Net Promoter Score)
 Survey metric measuring customer satisfaction and loyalty.
Negotiator/Closer
 Persona responsible for finalizing commercial terms and securing deal closure.

### O
Odoo CRM
 Sales management platform used for pipeline tracking, PFI generation, documentation, and lifecycle management.
Onboarding
 Process of training users, setting up configuration, and ensuring customer adopts the system.

### P
PFI (Proforma Invoice)
 Official quote sent to customers; includes hardware, subscription, labor, and ISS bundles.
PPMM (Price Product Market Matrix)
 Matrix defining product pricing, hardware mapping, and market segmentation.
PR Bands
 Performance rating bands (A–E) used to evaluate staff performance and determine commission multipliers.
Presenter
 Persona responsible for conducting demos and presentations.
PPE (Personal Protective Equipment)
 Safety equipment for CSOs in field installations.

### Q
QMS (Quality Management System)
 Governing framework of documented processes for compliance, audit readiness, SOP enforcement.

### R
Renewal Cycle
 Monthly or annual subscription continuation process.
Repeat Business
 Upsell or cross-sell to existing customers (e.g., add-ons, sensors, cameras, maintenance modules).
Roaming SIM
 SIM card operating outside home country incurring higher data costs.

### S
SLA (Service Level Agreement)
 Contract specifying service expectations, support obligations, response times, and delivery terms.
SQL (Sales Qualified Lead)
 Lead that meets BANT and is ready for demo or proposal.
Subscription Revenue
 Recurring monthly revenue (MRR) from tracking services.

### T
TA (Transport Analyst)
 Role repositioned for repeat business generation and customer account growth.
Telematics
 Technology for tracking vehicles, fuel, drivers, equipment, and staff in real-time.
Token Billing (Navas)
 Consumption-based billing structure used in Navas.

### U
Upsell / Cross-sell / Deep-sell
 Methods for increasing customer lifetime value by adding more solutions.
User Adoption
 Measure of how effectively a customer is using the system post-installation.

### V
VEBA
 Upcoming Navas module — a vehicle booking system (Uber-style).
Version Control
 Method for tracking revisions of documents (v1.0, v1.1, v2.0).

### W
Wialon
 Global telematics platform powering enterprise fleet management for 3D.
Workflow Automation
 Process-driven sequences handled by n8n or AI agents.
Work-from-Home Allowance
 Monthly airtime/data support for staff as defined in HR & Finance policies.
